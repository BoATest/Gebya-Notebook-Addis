How this works in practice:
• On shared phones, staff switch to their actor and submit from the same device
• On multi-device setups, staff submit from their own phone, owner reviews from theirs (sync pending)
• Every step is timestamped and attributed — full audit trail
• Once finalized, both parties have a permanent record they agreed to
